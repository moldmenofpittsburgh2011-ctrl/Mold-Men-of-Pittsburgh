Mold Mitigation Specialists

Surface cleaning doesn't stop mold. It spreads it. At Mold Men of Pittsburgh, our mitigation process is designed to eliminate mold completely — at the root level — so it doesn't come back three months later.

What Types of Mold Do We Remove?

  • Black mold (Stachybotrys) — Found in areas with chronic moisture. Serious health risk if left untreated.
  • Chaetomium — Often found in water-damaged drywall and roofing. Produces mycotoxins.
  • Aspergillus & Penicillium — Common indoor molds that grow fast and spread through HVAC systems.
  • Cladosporium — Often found on HVAC coils, fabric, and in crawl spaces.

Mold Assessment & Testing

Before you spend money on mold removal, you need accurate information. What type of mold is it? How far has it spread? What's the toxicity level?

Why Lab Testing Matters

A visual mold inspection tells you where mold is visible. Lab testing tells you everything else: the species, the concentration of spores in the air, the severity of contamination, and which rooms are affected even if nothing is visible yet. Mold hides in wall cavities, behind insulation, in HVAC ductwork, and under flooring. Lab results give you a full picture.

Our two-sample mold test is conducted by an independent third-party lab — not by us — so results are unbiased and legally defensible.
